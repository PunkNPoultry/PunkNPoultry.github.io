Before we got ducks we had chickens, so at the time I figured they’d just be basically big chickens that were good swimmers. And while I was prepared for the sometimes brutal sexual escapades that we’d already experienced with hen/rooster humping, NOBODY prepared me for the spectacle that is a duck’s penis. Note: We are going to use the technical term "penis" exactly once. From here on out, you never know what you’ll get.

See, most male birds (about 97%) are completely missing their outside plumbing and instead have a cloaca like their female counterpart. That’s literally their "poop chute", but it serves double duty as it’s also where eggs pop out.  Chickens do the dirty via a "cloacal kiss", which is like chickens doing a fist bump, except awkwardly and with their butts. It only lasts a few seconds, is still very effective and there is ZERO "romance". On any average day, our roosters chase the girls around, committing multiple felonies in our backyard.

But ducks … aah, my adorable little noisemakers\! Drakes are blessed with an appendage that looks like fusilli pasta or a curly fry. So I guess the ducks looked at the chickens' perfectly serviceable and time-efficient system and said "let's make it *worse*."

Ducks are just about the cutest things on the planet, up until the time that the males start feeling romantic. (Honestly, duck dick may be the reason I have trust issues.) Unexpected lessons on our farm are not uncommon, but that year I got the mother of all lessons watching a drake “finish” with one of his ladies and proceed to drag his biological bottle opener around for a full minute before pulling it back up into his booty. Thankfully we’d seen this before (pigs have a drill bit doodad too), so we didn’t panic or start calling vets.
But we gained a newfound respect for Mother Nature’s hustle.

Where there is a will, there is a way. Duck female anatomy is made to handle the corkscrew, so it’s just as complex but unseen. And in about a month, we added more ducklings to the mix.  

If you start keeping male ducks,  you will see the spiral surprise eventually and after a while it won’t be as disturbing. Unless it remains out for hours, in which case you should probably call a vet.

Otherwise, don’t worry about the drake dangler. We told the kids it’s just nature’s way of saying [Helloooo nurse\!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tPx_c_QbbUM).
